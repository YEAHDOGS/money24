## JEV QUANT AI

As filed in the project backlog (highest priority on the money24 board):

> TypeSafe, cheap, ludicrously fast, deterministic AI — the secret key to
> getting 24/7 quant AI trading bots up and running. Dive into everything
> Jev, reverse engineer, and use to make AI trading bots ASAP.

Status: **lead only, not yet researched.** The interesting questions when
it's picked up: what the Jev stack actually is, whether "deterministic"
holds up (determinism is the property that would matter most for a trading
loop — same inputs, same decision, auditable after the fact), what it costs
to run, and whether it beats the current agent design on speed or is a
rewrite for its own sake. The engine build stop order applies: research is
fine, building waits for the owner's word.
